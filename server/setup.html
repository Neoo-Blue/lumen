Set up your assistant

A few minutes, once. Everything stays on this server and your phone; nothing is open to the internet.

1 Give it a name

What you will call it, and what it calls itself.

2 Choose its brain

The AI model that thinks for it. You bring your own key; it is stored only on this server.

Use a plan you already pay for. The server runs that company's own app, signed in as you. Personal use only; check your plan's terms.


        

      
Signed in on your own computer instead?
        
Paste the tool's sign-in file here: Claude Code: the token from claude setup-token. Codex: ~/.codex/auth.json. Gemini CLI: ~/.gemini/oauth_creds.json. OpenCode: ~/.local/share/opencode/auth.json.
